## Taxation
### When is GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B due?
- GSTR-1: 11th of every month (for monthly taxpayers).
- GSTR-3B: 20th of every month.

### What are the due dates for ITR?
- Non-audit cases (individuals, salaried, proprietorship): 31st July.
- Audit cases (companies, audited firms): 31st October.
